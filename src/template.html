Email Hasher

Client-side hashing and comparison for advocacy-org list swaps.

Your emails never leave your browser. This page works offline. How do I verify?

This page makes zero network requests after it loads. You can confirm that yourself:

  1. Open your browser's DevTools (⌘/Ctrl + Shift + I), click the Network tab, reload the page, then hash a file. The list should stay empty.
  2. Or put your computer in airplane mode and run the tool — everything still works.
  3. Or download the release bundle and open index.html from a folder on your computer.

Drop your CSV here, or click to choose a file.

Case normalization

Both orgs must use the exact same passphrase. Share it out-of-band — in person, by phone, or Signal — not over email.
